## 1. Approval status summary

### Already approved outcomes: IT-01–IT-15

The approved requirements are preserved in the identity ADR. In brief: immutable PARMAR account identity (IT-01); defer provider selection (IT-02); optional method linking only with fresh proof and no automatic linking/merging (IT-03); secure recovery and abuse protection (IT-04); per-device sessions, inventory, logout, and step-up where appropriate (IT-05); eventual personal/multiple-profile/family-team scope with separate enablement gates (IT-06); workspace membership does not grant personal-content access (IT-07); owner-controlled profiles with explicit, revocable View/Edit/Manage grants (IT-08); user-scoped memory by default (IT-09); user controls and truthful lifecycle reporting (IT-10); production data/backup protection requirements (IT-11); scoped external-AI consent and no silent fallback (IT-12); PARMAR-managed AI as a gated long-term goal (IT-13); offline/sync safeguards including no automatic side-effect replay (IT-14); and secure closure, revocation, truthful deletion reporting, and no standing support access to private content (IT-15).

These are approved outcomes, not proof of implementation, security acceptance, or production readiness. Detailed mechanisms, thresholds, retention periods, and release gates remain open where noted in the ADR and traceability matrix.

### Already approved product directions: D-01–D-13

The [Master PRD decision register](./VOKKI-MASTER-PRD.md) already approves the product directions: D-01 web-first with independent platform gates; D-02 one PARMAR-wide account/SSO boundary with immutable account IDs and strict isolation; D-03 hybrid AI with scoped consent and no silent fallback; D-04 user-controlled memory; D-05 explicit user-initiated camera capture; D-06 multilingual voice with wake-word separately gated; D-07 preservation of visual directions while renderer choice remains open; D-08 PARMAR Core governance for actions, with approval and verification gates for side effects; D-09 controlled offline drafts/sync and no side-effect replay; D-10 accessibility and multilingual targets; D-11 research provenance and independent verification; D-12 gated PARMAR-managed AI direction; and D-13 user-controlled customization with canonical identity and asset controls.

The approved D-02 shared identity/SSO boundary is **not a pending decision in this register**. Technical provider/protocol selection and each product's integration and release remain separately gated.

### Existing architecture constraints

- The Task 37 registry is an inactive v3 persistence foundation. `TrustedInternalAccountProvisioner` has no application caller or public endpoint and is not an enforced in-process capability. The provider-binding repository is read-only; there is no active provider adapter or binding writer. See [Core Identity Contracts](./PARMAR-CORE-IDENTITY-CONTRACTS.md).
- The registry currently has `ACTIVE`/`DISABLED` records, not the proposed full lifecycle state machine. Proposed states and transitions below are not implemented.
- Account identity is PARMAR-generated, random UUIDv4, immutable, provider-independent, and not client-selected. No automatic account linking, merging, email/name matching, or unapproved legacy-ID mapping.
- Home and VOKKI currently retain legacy `Principal.user_id` paths; no live account-registry resolver or approved migration is established. Product authorization remains separate from authentication.
- Workspace membership does not grant access to personal content. Memory remains user-scoped by default; unassigned legacy conversations remain unassigned absent a separately approved migration.
- AETHER Desktop `REMOTE_BROWSING_ENABLED=false` remains unchanged. AETHER Android remains strictly offline-only in its current release.

### Approved proposed policy baseline

APR-01–APR-20 record approval of the recommended policy direction at the proposed-policy-baseline level. Unresolved operational details, numeric thresholds, provider selection, legal/privacy review, and all security and implementation gates remain open. None changes IT-01–IT-15 or D-01–D-13.

The approved baseline includes open, user-initiated PARMAR self-service registration; invitations are not mandatory. Desired login methods are Google, Apple, Email OTP, and Email + Password; additional providers may be evaluated for future support. Provider selection, provider integration, and production activation remain subject to IT-02 and all stated security gates. Each account receives a unique PARMAR-issued Account ID; matching email addresses never automatically merge accounts. PARMAR-wide SSO remains the approved architecture direction, with product-specific authorization and session controls mandatory.

This direction does not claim that production authentication, registration, or any of these methods is implemented. Preserve secure recovery, passkey support, rate limits, per-device session revocation, action-specific step-up verification, durable security auditing, fail-closed handling when required audit recording fails, and the distinction between authentication, account identity, and authorization. Preserve user controls for review, export, correction, deletion, and reset; class-specific retention and backup/replica lifecycle requirements; and workspace/personal-data isolation. There is no standing support access to personal content; any exception remains purpose-limited, time-bound, least-privilege, and audited.

The proposed 30-day closure grace period remains a candidate only and requires legal, privacy, and operational validation. It is not a finalized or universal retention rule. No immediate deletion from every backup or external-provider copy is promised.

### Production implementation/security gates still outstanding

No production provisioning, authentication, provider integration, binding writer, SSO, recovery, or cross-product rollout is authorized. Before any dependent capability:

1. Approve applicable policy decisions below and assign accountable policy/security/privacy/legal/operations owners.
2. Complete requirements-based provider comparison and security review before selecting a provider or protocol.
3. Approve authentication assurance, recovery, abuse, session/device, account lifecycle, and provider-binding contracts.
4. Approve complete server-side route/resource authorization, data-class lifecycle, and product-specific audience/integration boundaries.
5. Implement only under a separately authorized task; test persistence, races, replay, revocation, isolation, failures, recovery, deletion reporting, and platform boundaries.
6. Obtain independent security/privacy review and explicit release approval on the exact deployment and supported platform artifacts.

## 3. Already-settled decisions — not reopened by this register

1. **PARMAR-wide shared identity/SSO boundary:** D-02 is approved. PARMAR products use one shared PARMAR account identity; Home and VOKKI are separate product consumers under PARMAR Core, not separate identity systems, and no separate VOKKI/AETHER account or login namespace is created. This register does not ask whether to adopt that boundary. Provider/protocol, SSO transport, session handoff, and product rollout remain gated.
2. **Immutable PARMAR account identity:** IT-01 is approved. PARMAR issues random provider-independent UUIDv4 account IDs; clients and providers do not choose them.
3. **No automatic linking, merging, or email-based matching:** IT-03 is approved. Optional method linking requires fresh proof and explicit user intent. No matching email/name/phone, UUID equality, or legacy-ID inference establishes identity.
4. **Workspace membership does not grant personal-content access:** IT-07 is approved. Product/resource authorization remains distinct from authentication; explicit profile grants remain owner-controlled under IT-08.
5. **Provider selection remains deferred:** IT-02 is approved. Candidate comparison and security approval precede any selection/integration. This register selects no provider.
6. **AETHER Android remains offline-only in its current release:** no Internet permission, networking implementation, WebView, remote authentication, or SSO. A future change would require a distinct decision and review.
7. **AETHER Desktop remote browsing remains disabled:** `REMOTE_BROWSING_ENABLED=false`; authentication does not grant browsing authority or relax independent Desktop isolation gates.
8. **PARMAR governance and human approval remain separate from identity:** D-08 and the approved action/approval boundaries are unchanged. Authentication or account status alone never authorizes a sensitive action, side effect, or execution.
