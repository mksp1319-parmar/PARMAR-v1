## Follow-up milestone key

- **V2 — Production identity and route perimeter:** provider-neutral account/SSO contract, candidate-comparison/security-approval process, identity/recovery/session policy, and complete route authorization inventory.
- **V3 — Consent and data lifecycle:** memory consent, export/delete/reset, encryption/key management, backups, residency/recovery, account closure, and provider deletion limits.
- **V4 — Governed conversation and provider egress:** approved inference path, explicit user-scoped transfer consent, minimization, transport, zero-egress denial, and no fallback.
- **V5 — Memory, research and files:** scoped authorization and lifecycle for each data class, including profile/workspace grant enforcement.
- **V8 — Offline/hybrid and sync:** offline data classes, encryption, explicit conflict handling, reauthorization, revocation, deletion, and no-replay acceptance.

Milestone mapping follows [PARALLEL-DEVELOPMENT-MASTER-PLAN.md](./PARALLEL-DEVELOPMENT-MASTER-PLAN.md); the detailed choices and accountable owners remain to be approved. The matrix is a documentation index, not a complete route inventory, implementation authorization, or production acceptance report.
