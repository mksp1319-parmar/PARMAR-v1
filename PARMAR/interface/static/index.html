        How memory is stored and used

        Authenticated notes are stored durably on this server’s local disk only after explicit consent and an explicit Save action. Storage is not encrypted at rest; notes remain until you delete them, with limits of 25 notes and 400 characters per note. Relevant consented notes may be used only as untrusted context in authenticated Chat/VOKKI; if an external provider is selected and authorized, matched notes may be included in its request. Local-demo notes remain separate in this browser. Do not save passwords, tokens, private keys, or other secrets.
